Take a little walk

Yes, this is an ordinary webpage.

Minimize RIFT using its close button. Your companion can roam the edges of this page, and follow you as you scroll. Drag them to a new spot, click for a nose boop, or double-click to return to the sanctuary.

On other websites, use Settings → Companion on this site to invite them onto the page. The small RIFT button opens your sanctuary wherever userscripts are allowed.

Ready to take them with you?

Install RIFT-Cozy-Companion.user.js in Tampermonkey. This preview and the installed script have separate saves; use Settings → Export save here, then import the file into the installed script to carry your companion over.

No external assets, accounts, or network requests. The complete experience is inside this file.
